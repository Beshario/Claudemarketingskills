# Hero Video — Shoot Day Checklist

*Created: 2026-05-30 | Pair with [first-session-wow.md](./first-session-wow.md) §4 (the beat-by-beat).*

Concrete pre-flight, capture sequence, and gotchas so the takes are clean and the glow→recede
animation lands. Goal: usable footage in one sitting, no reshoot loop.

---

## Pre-flight (5–10 min)

- **Build:** the prototype with the animation trigger wired on submit + "Choose Template" as
  the first action in Plan. Verify the cycle fires once manually before recording.
- **Account state:** fresh user **or** an existing user with **no prior workouts logged today**,
  so the Day View body map reflects *only* this session. Carry-over heat masks the wow.
- **Theme:** pick one and commit — light or dark. Dark reads more cinematic against the
  signature orange ramp; light reads more accessible/approachable. Confirm whichever matches the
  cover/thumbnail in [first-session-promise-copy.md](./first-session-promise-copy.md) §4.
- **Device:** Pixel/test Android with full battery, brightness up, **DND on**, notifications
  silenced. Airplane mode if it doesn't break anything needed.
- **Recorder:** device-native screen recorder at max resolution, **60fps** if available.
  Save direct to device storage — no cloud-routed recorder, no compression in the path.
- **Stage the calendar:** wipe any cluttering planned workouts that show in the Plan/Day View
  during the capture. The frame should feel uncluttered.
- **Pick the template ahead of time.** Choose one that creates **clear hot/cold contrast** on
  the body map — e.g. an upper-body push or back-focused session. Avoid full-body templates
  for the hero cut — they flood the heatmap and the wow loses contrast.

---

## Capture sequence (~45–60s, one continuous take)

1. **Open app** → land on the Plan tab. *(Beat 1)*
2. **Tap "Choose Template"** (or whatever the precise label is — confirm).
3. **Pick the staged template.** Hold for 1s after it loads. *(Beat 2–3)*
4. **Log sets** on the first exercise — weight, reps, RIR. Use realistic, round numbers;
   no fumbling. *(Beat 4)*
5. **Hit submit** → "Complete workout? (N sets)" toast → **Submit**. *(Beat 5)*
6. **Land on Day View.** **HOLD STEADY** through the entire glow→recede cycle. No pan, no
   scroll, no tap. This is the wow — let the animation play out fully. *(Beats 6–7)*
7. After it settles, hold the settled body map another 1–2 seconds. *(Beat 8)*
8. Stop recording.

**Record the whole flow at least twice.** First pass for the take, second to refine pacing or
fix anything. Editing is cheaper than re-shoot setup.

---

## Watch-outs

- **Don't tap before the animation completes.** The glow→recede cycle is the wow; cutting it
  short kills the beat.
- **Avoid full-body templates** — even glow over every muscle reads as nothing.
- **Keep cursor/finger out of frame** during the Day View hold.
- **Notification banners or system prompts** (battery, OS) mid-capture = re-shoot. Pre-empt
  with DND + airplane mode if possible.
- **No date confusion.** Submitting a future-dated workout aborts with a toast (per code) —
  shoot against today.
- **Round numbers when logging** — "100 / 8 / 2" reads cleanly on overlay; "97.5 / 7 / 1.5"
  looks chaotic in close-up.
