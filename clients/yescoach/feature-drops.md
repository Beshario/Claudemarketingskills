# YesCoach — Feature Drops (content log)

*Created: 2026-06-02 | Purpose: track per-feature mini-content as the app ships.*

Each shipped feature gets a short BIP/demo asset (15–30s vertical video typical). These are
**secondary content** — the hero activation video ([first-session-wow.md](./first-session-wow.md))
stays the lead acquisition asset. Feature drops feed the build-in-public spine and the
roadmap-credibility signal from [knowledge-context.md](./knowledge-context.md).

**Guardrail (from knowledge-context.md):** lead with strength tracking + intelligence as the
hero brand. Drops for mobility, recovery, etc. signal the expansion vision ("Google Maps for
the human body") without pivoting the brand promise. Don't position YesCoach as "the mobility
app" — position mobility as the *platform expanding*.

### Positioning note

This drop signals **expansion**, validates the roadmap, and proves the engine generalizes
beyond strength — without claiming category leadership in mobility. Mobility is a new room in
the same house, not a pivot. Keep strength + intelligence in the foreground; let mobility be
the *proof* that the body-map substrate works for more than logging lifts.

Honest tell on the YouTube handoff: it's a feature, not a gap. Frame it as utility ("form
reference, one tap away") — *we don't reinvent the wheel; we plug you in.* Don't hide it.

- **X:** post mid-week as the Show beat in the 60/30/10 ratio. Don't pin — the activation
