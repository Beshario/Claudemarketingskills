## 6. Promise alignment rules (keep these tight across all surfaces)

- **Do say:** *pick a ready plan · train · submit · body map lights up with what you worked.*
- **Don't lead with:** *"tap any muscle and build your own workout"* (true, but the pro path —
  it's the friction that makes newcomers bounce; save it for the power-user cut).
- **Frame the body map as a mirror:** *here's your session*. Never *here's what you neglected*.
- **One promise per surface.** Don't dilute with feature lists. The wow is the promise.
- **Premium tier** (weekly/monthly map, muscle tracking over time, recovery): roadmap signal,
  not the first-session promise. Mention it after the user has logged a session.
